Things you may be wondering

What does the training process look like?

We come to you, your home, your yard or a nearby park. Most programs run one session a week with your trainer plus about 15 minutes of daily practice, across a handful of private sessions depending on your goals.

What should we expect from the training?

Reliable obedience that holds up in the real world: following commands, staying in position until released, and ignoring distractions. Programs are personalized to your dog and your goals, and you work with the same trainer throughout.

Whether it's puppy biting, leash pulling, reactivity, separation anxiety or simply not listening, we can help.

What is your training approach?

A balanced approach: clear rewards, clear boundaries, and the least pressure needed to change behavior. You train alongside us so the results hold long after we leave.

What is the difference between the service dog and the therapy dog program?

A service dog is trained to perform specific tasks for one person and can accompany them almost anywhere, including on flights. A therapy dog is a well-mannered, friendly dog certified to bring comfort to others in hospitals, schools and care facilities, and doesn't have the same access rights.

Can every dog be successful in your program?

Yes. Every dog can learn obedience regardless of age, breed or temperament. Some progress faster than others, and that's completely normal.
