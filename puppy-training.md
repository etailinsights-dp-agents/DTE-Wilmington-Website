## Build good habits from the start

The first months of a puppy's life are an ideal time to establish communication, boundaries and dependable behaviors. We'll help you teach your puppy what you want them to do, not simply correct unwanted behavior after it develops.

Training can address potty training, puppy biting, jumping, leash manners, basic commands, crate behavior and other common puppy challenges.

## What we help puppy owners with

- **Potty training.** A consistent schedule, well-timed trips outside and rewards for getting it right, so accidents taper off instead of becoming a habit.
- **Biting and nipping.** Mouthing is normal puppy behavior. We teach gentle mouth manners and give you a plan for redirecting those sharp teeth to the right outlets.
- **Crate training.** Building the crate into a calm, comfortable place your puppy goes willingly, which helps with house training, naps and settling when you're away.
- **Leash walking.** Loose-leash habits taught early, before pulling becomes the way your puppy expects every walk to work.
- **Basic obedience.** Name recognition, sit, down, come and stay, built in short, positive sessions that fit a puppy's attention span.
- **Jumping on people.** A polite greeting routine, so your puppy learns that four paws on the floor is what earns attention. Our guide to [stopping jumping and leash pulling](/resources/stop-dog-jumping-on-guests-leash-pulling/) covers the basics.
- **Socialization.** Calm, positive exposure to new people, sounds, surfaces and places while your puppy is at the age when new experiences are easiest to accept.
- **Time alone.** Practicing short, comfortable separations so your puppy learns to settle when the house is quiet.
